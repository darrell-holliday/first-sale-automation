Subject or first line: Good talking today

## Day 2

Darrell

## Day 5

Hi {name},

I'll keep this short. The offer we talked about is still open if you want to run through it on a call: {link}

Darrell

## Day 10, last note

Last note from me on this. If you want to pick it up later, email me. I'll leave you alone otherwise.
